Bridging the Silent Divide

Over 70 million individuals worldwide communicate primarily through sign language. In daily civilian scenarios — seeking medical care, commercial transactions, or public transit — non-signers are unable to comprehend gestures, isolating hearing and speech-impaired persons.

During my research internship at Teerthanker Mahaveer University, under the guidance of Dr. Rakesh Dwivedi, I engineered a cost-effective, standalone sensory glove that decodes hand positions and speaks aloud in English and Hindi.

ENGINEERING HIGHLIGHTS
  • 5 conductive flex resistors measuring finger deflection 0°–90°
  • 6-axis gyroscope/accelerometer tracking spatial orientation
  • Low-latency Bluetooth 5.0 wireless smartphone sync
  • Built-in DFPlayer Mini voice synthesis module
