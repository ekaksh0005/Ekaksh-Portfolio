01 / PROBLEM CONTEXT

The Bottleneck in Global Pandemic Preparedness

The COVID-19 pandemic exposed humanity's severe vulnerability to emerging infectious pathogens. Millions of lives were lost and billions confined to their homes while international laboratories raced against time. A fundamental cause behind the dangerous latency in vaccine development was the complex, computationally grueling process of solving the 3D atomic structure of viral surface proteins — particularly the SARS-CoV-2 spike protein that mediates host cell entry via ACE2 receptors.

When a novel virus is identified, isolating and sequencing its genetic code is merely the first step. The genetic sequence dictates an amino acid polypeptide chain that collapses into an intricate 3D topology. Because structure dictates biological function, resolving these folds is the non-negotiable prerequisite to designing targeted neutralizing antibodies, small-molecule therapeutics, and mRNA vaccine immunogens.

KEY TECHNICAL OBJECTIVES
  • Direct amino-acid sequence to 3D coordinate mapping
  • Acceleration over classical brute-force molecular dynamics
  • High-fidelity modeling of viral spikes, TB enzymes & envelope proteins
  • Quantum sorting evaluation of multi-state folding energy landscapes
02 / CORE METHODOLOGY

Quantum Superposition Meets Deep Learning

Conventional algorithms evaluate conformational energy states sequentially: accessing each discrete configuration in an array one by one ($O(N)$ operations). In contrast, Project Vriti leverages Quantum Sorting principles where a quantum register explores superpositions across the entire conformational state space simultaneously ($O(\sqrt{N})$ complexity via quantum parallelism).

// Quantum State Superposition Formulation for Fold Evaluation:
|Ψ_protein⟩ = (1 / √2^n) ∑_{k=0}^{2^n - 1} |Residue_k⟩ ⊗ |Φ_torsion(φ, ψ)⟩
Energy Oracle: U_E |Residue_k⟩|0⟩ → |Residue_k⟩|E(Residue_k)⟩
Quantum Sorting searches global energy minima across all torsion angles simultaneously.
SPATIAL PATTERN RECOGNITION

Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs)

CNN layers parse the residue-residue distance and contact maps, identifying recurring spatial secondary structures (alpha-helices, beta-pleated sheets, and hairpin turns) across 3D Cartesian coordinates.

LONG-RANGE SEQUENCE DEPENDENCIES

Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs)

RNN architectures (LSTM/GRU) process the sequential linear sequence of amino acids (often exceeding 500+ residues), preserving non-local electrostatic and hydrophobic bonding context along the polypeptide backbone.
